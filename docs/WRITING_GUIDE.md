# Writing guide

Use short, clear statements in documentation, UI copy, errors, comments, and delivery notes. These principles are inspired by Simplified Technical English; they do not claim certified compliance.

- Use one term for each concept: medicine, batch, plan, dose, episode, and observation.
- Name the subject and action. State a condition before its dependent action.
- Give errors a useful recovery action without disclosing private data.
- Describe recorded facts separately from interpretations and uncertainty.
- Remove labels that repeat information already clear from the layout.
- Keep medically important qualifications, units, and provenance visible.
- Respect the conventions of each language. Do not force English rules onto Chinese.
- Support completion claims with executed checks and their limits.
